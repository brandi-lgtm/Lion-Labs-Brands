The opportunity

This is not a general awareness campaign.

Lion Labs already has meaningful recognition in Michigan. The next stage is national: reaching established operators in new markets that have the licenses, manufacturing capabilities, distribution, and sales strength required to bring the portfolio into market successfully.

Reach qualified operators

Place Lion Labs in front of owners, executives, manufacturers, distributors, and multi-state decision-makers already working within regulated cannabis.

Explain the business model

Move beyond product awareness and show how a differentiated house of brands can expand an operator's portfolio without requiring every brand to be built from the ground up.

Prioritize fit

Use a purpose-built qualification path so Lion Labs can identify partners with the market access and operational strength it wants.

The strategy

Built around the outcome.

The campaign gives direct outreach stronger air cover. When Lion Labs approaches a prospective operator, that company can find a credible profile, understand the partnership model, and move into a clear qualification process.
